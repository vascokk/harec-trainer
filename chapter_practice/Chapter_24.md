### 24.1 Allocation of Call Signs

**1.** Irish amateur call signs are issued:

- A) For the lifetime of the licensee
- B) For 5 years
- C) For 1 year
- D) For each contact

**2.** When may an Irish amateur's call sign change?

- A) When buying a new radio
- B) When moving house
- C) Every 5 years
- D) When changing from a CEPT Class 2 to a Class 1 licence

**3.** What happens to a call sign when a licence is surrendered or cancelled?

- A) It is kept for 5 years
- B) It is given to the next applicant
- C) It is permanently revoked and never reissued
- D) It passes to the family

### 24.2 Composition of Call Signs

**4.** A normal amateur call sign consists of:

- A) Any six letters
- B) A 1–2 character nationality prefix, a single digit, and a group of up to four characters ending in a letter
- C) Three digits and three letters
- D) A name and a number

**5.** Which call sign is correctly formed?

- A) 2EABCD
- B) EI4RGD7
- C) EI6XYZ
- D) 26A

**6.** Why is 2EABCD not a valid call sign?

- A) It has no single digit after the prefix
- B) It ends in a letter
- C) It is too short
- D) It starts with a digit

**7.** Why is 2-6-A not a valid call sign?

- A) It ends in a letter
- B) The prefix has no letter
- C) It has a digit
- D) It is too long

### 24.3 Irish Call Signs

**8.** Which is a valid normal Irish call sign?

- A) EI33RDB

**9.** When operating from an Irish offshore island, EI3F becomes:

- A) EJ/EI3F
- B) EI3F/P
- C) EJ3F

**10.** Who may be issued a distinctive call sign such as EI90IRTS?

- A) Anyone who asks on air
- B) Special event stations, by ComReg
- C) Only contest stations
- D) Only foreign visitors

### 24.4 Call Sign Usage

**11.** Which call sign suffixes are allowed by Irish regulations?

- A) Any suffix
- B) /P and /QRP
- C) /M, /P and /MM
- D) Only /M and /MM

**12.** How is the suffix /M pronounced according to ComReg guidelines?

- A) Slash mobile
- B) Portable
- C) Mike
- D) Maritime

**13.** When operating /M, the call sign and location must be sent at the start and end of each contact, or every:

- A) 60 minutes
- B) 5 minutes
- C) 30 minutes, whichever is more frequent
- D) 10 minutes

## Answer Key

| Q | Ans | Explanation (Study Guide page) |
|---|-----|-------------|
| 1 | A | Call signs only change when moving from Class 2 to Class 1 (p. 336) |
| 2 | D | That is the only occasion (p. 336) |
| 3 | C | Revoked call signs are not reissued (p. 336) |
| 4 | B | Prefix (at least one letter), one digit, then up to four characters ending in a letter (p. 336) |
| 5 | C | EI4RGD7 ends in a digit; 2EABCD has no digit; 26A has no letter in the prefix (p. 336) |
| 6 | A | Section 2 must be a single digit (p. 336) |
| 7 | B | The nationality prefix must contain at least one letter (p. 336) |
| 8 | D | EI, one digit, and a 1–4 letter suffix (p. 337) |
| 9 | C | No application needed: change EI to EJ (p. 337) |
| 10 | B | Special event call signs may not follow the normal rules (p. 337) |
| 11 | D | /P and /QRP are not permitted (p. 337) |
| 12 | A | ComReg specifies "slash"; the IARU guide recommends "stroke" (p. 337) |
| 13 | C | The same rule applies to /MM (p. 337) |
