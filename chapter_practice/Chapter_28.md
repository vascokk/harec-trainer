### 28.1 Basic Principles

**1.** Which is one of the basic principles of the amateur radio code of conduct?

- A) Ignoring other operators
- B) Winning every argument on air
- C) Using strong language to make a point
- D) Tolerance of people and their differing opinions

**2.** You hear another amateur say something technically wrong on air. The best response is to:

- A) Jam the frequency
- B) Ridicule them
- C) Offer polite, constructive explanation
- D) Report them to ComReg

**3.** Why should you always remain polite and avoid rude language on air?

- A) It costs extra
- B) Everyone can listen, even people not in the conversation
- C) Rude words reduce signal strength
- D) Only Morse is monitored

### 28.5 Amateur Radio Language
