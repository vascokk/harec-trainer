### 26.1 Q-Codes

**1.** Q-codes are:

- A) Standard three-letter codes beginning with Q
- B) Call sign prefixes
- C) ITU emission designators
- D) Morse prosigns only

**2.** QRG? means:

- A) Should I stop?
- B) Are you busy?
- C) What is your location?
- D) What is the (exact) frequency?

**3.** QRK? means:

- A) Is my signal fading?
- B) Are you ready?
- C) What is the readability of my signals?
- D) Who is calling me?

**4.** QRL? means:

- A) Should I increase power?
- B) Is the frequency busy?
- C) Can you confirm reception?
- D) What is your location?

**5.** QRM means:

- A) I am bothered by atmospherics
- B) I am being interfered with
- C) Your signal is fading
- D) Increase your power

**6.** QRN means:

- A) I am ready
- B) I am being interfered with by another station
- C) I am bothered by atmospherics (natural noise)
- D) I have nothing for you

**7.** QRO means:

- A) Send slower
- B) Decrease your power
- C) Stop transmitting
- D) Increase your power

**8.** QRP means:

- A) Decrease your power
- B) Increase your power
- C) I am ready
- D) Change frequency

**9.** QRS means:

- A) Repeat your call sign
- B) Stop your transmission
- C) Increase your speed
- D) Decrease your sending speed

**10.** QRT means:

- A) Wait
- B) Decrease your power
- C) Stop your transmission
- D) Change frequency

**11.** QRU means:

- A) I have nothing for you
- B) I am ready
- C) I will call you back
- D) You are called by

**12.** QRV means:

- A) I am busy
- B) I am ready
- C) Wait
- D) Your signal is fading

**13.** QRX means:

- A) Who is calling me?
- B) Stop transmitting
- C) Increase power
- D) I will call you back at …; wait, stand by

**14.** QRZ? means:

- A) Is the frequency busy?
- B) What is your location?
- C) Who is (was) calling me?
- D) Are you ready?

**15.** QSB means:

- A) I confirm reception
- B) Your signal is fading
- C) Change frequency
- D) I am being interfered with

**16.** QSL means:

- A) I confirm reception
- B) Your signal is fading
- C) Stop transmitting
- D) My location is

**17.** QSY means:

- A) Listen on …
- B) Change frequency to …
- C) Wait
- D) Decrease power

**18.** QSX means:

- A) Stop
- B) Transmit on …
- C) Listen on …
- D) Confirm

**19.** QTH means:

- A) My location is …
- B) My name is …
- C) My power is …
- D) My frequency is …

**20.** If you hear QUF, you should:

- A) Increase power
- B) Reply with your QTH
- C) Change frequency
- D) Stop transmitting, listen and follow any instructions; it is an emergency signal

### 26.2 Q-Code as a Question or an Answer

**21.** In telegraphy, how is a Q-code made into a question?

- A) By adding K
- B) By sending it twice
- C) By adding a question mark after the code
- D) By sending it slowly

**22.** In telephony, a Q-code is made into a question by:

- A) Spelling it phonetically
- B) Speaking it with a questioning tone of voice
- C) Saying it three times
- D) Adding "over"

**23.** You send QRL? and receive QRL. This means:

- A) Yes, the frequency is in use
- B) The frequency is free
- C) Please increase power
- D) Change frequency

### 26.3 Operational Abbreviations

**24.** The abbreviation CQ means:

- A) End of contact
- B) A call to a specific station
- C) Please repeat
- D) A general call to all stations

**25.** The abbreviation DE means:

- A) Delete
- B) Distress
- C) From; separates the called station's call sign from the caller's
- D) Distance

**26.** The abbreviation DX means:

- A) Long distance, usually another continent
- B) Direct transmission
- C) Digital exchange
- D) Duplex

**27.** At the end of a Morse transmission, K means:

- A) End of contact
- B) Over: an invitation for the other operator to transmit
- C) Okay
- D) Kilowatt

**28.** The abbreviation BK means:

- A) Bureau
- B) Band
- C) Back soon
- D) A signal used to interrupt a transmission in progress

**29.** The abbreviation SKED means:

- A) Skip distance
- B) A scheduled call, planned and agreed ahead
- C) Sky wave
- D) Speed key

**30.** Which pair of abbreviations means transmitter and receiver?

- A) TX and RX
- B) TR and RE
- C) TS and RS
- D) XT and XR

**31.** The abbreviations PSE and UR mean:

- A) Pass and ready
- B) Pause and urgent
- C) Power supply and unit
- D) Please and your

**32.** Used on its own, R means:

- A) Radio
- B) Repeat
- C) Received; a general yes or confirmed
- D) Ready
